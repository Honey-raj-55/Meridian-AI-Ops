COVERAGE TIERS
================================================================================

TRAVELERS BASIC (Equivalent to HO3 bare minimum)
- Dwelling: RCV with 25% extended replacement cost
- Other structures: 10% of dwelling
- Personal property: ACV
- Loss of use: 20% of dwelling, 12 months
- Liability: $100,000
- Medical payments: $1,000
- Deductible: 1% named windstorm/hail; $1,000 all-other-peril
- Water backup: NOT included (available add-on $40-$80/yr)
- Roof: ACV on roofs 13+ years
- Suitable for: Investment/rental homes, price-only buyers; C1 rarely leads with this tier

TRAVELERS STANDARD (Most common C1 placement)
- Dwelling: RCV with 30% extended replacement cost
- Other structures: 10% of dwelling
- Personal property: RCV, $2,500 sublimit on jewelry
- Loss of use: 40% of dwelling, 18 months
- Liability: $300,000
- Medical payments: $5,000
- Deductible: 1% named windstorm/hail; $1,000 all-other-peril
- Water backup: $25,000 (included - stronger than Safeco Standard)
- Identity fraud: $25,000 (included)
- Roof: RCV roofs 0-16 years; ACV schedule 17-22 years; ineligible 23+
- Green rebuild: Travelers includes up to $25,000 for energy-efficient upgrades after a covered loss (unique feature)
- Best for: Mid-tier Dallas suburbs, clients with one prior claim, homes $300K-$700K

TRAVELERS PREMIER (IntelliDrive Home package)
- Dwelling: Guaranteed Replacement Cost - true GRC with no cap, confirmed via Xactimate estimate at bind
- Other structures: 20% of dwelling
- Personal property: RCV, scheduled jewelry/art/collectibles available with no sublimit
- Loss of use: 60% of dwelling, 24 months (strongest in DFW market)
- Liability: $500,000; umbrella integration up to $10M through Travelers Umbrella
- Medical payments: $10,000
- Deductible: 0.5% named windstorm/hail option; $500 all-other-peril option
- Water backup: $50,000
- Identity fraud: $50,000 with credit monitoring
- Equipment breakdown: $100,000 (best in class - covers HVAC, appliances, smart home systems)
- Ordinance/law: 100% of dwelling (critical for Dallas homes pre-2000)
- Valuable items floater: Included up to $10,000 jewelry/watches, $5,000 fine art
- Best for: Homes $500K+, custom/luxury builds, clients wanting Travelers umbrella continuity

TARGET CLIENT PROFILE - TRAVELERS SWEET SPOT
================================================================================

Ideal Travelers client at C1:
- Home value: $350,000 - $1,500,000 (Travelers' upper limit is higher than Safeco)
- Roof age: 0-16 years for Standard; 0-20 years for Premier with metal roof
- Credit score: 660+ (Travelers' scoring model is slightly more lenient than Safeco on mid-range scores)
- Claims history: Up to 1 paid claim under $40,000 in past 5 years on Standard; underwriting review on 2 claims
- Occupation: Executive, attorney, physician, small business owner (qualifies for IntelliDrive Home Premier eligibility)
- Auto bundle: Travelers' own auto policy gets 12-15% multi-policy discount; NOT as deep as Safeco/Liberty bundle but cleaner for umbrella stacking
- Construction: Frame, brick-veneer, full brick, ICF (insulated concrete form) - Travelers accepts more construction types than Safeco
- Pool/amenity: Travelers is more accommodating on pools, sports courts, guest houses with proper liability limits

Dallas-specific notes:

- ZIP 75230, 75225, 75248 (Preston Hollow, Far North Dallas): Strong Travelers territory; Premier tier common here
- Lakewood/Junius Heights (ZIP 75214): Travelers accepts the older stock with ordinance/law endorsement; Safeco often declines these
- Inner Oak Cliff, Bishop Arts (ZIP 75208, 75211): Travelers will write Standard with inspection; verify construction year
- Lake Ray Hubbard area (Rowlett, Rockwall): Travelers comfortable here; some wind exposure but manageable

DALLAS HAIL AND ROOF-AGE UNDERWRITING NOTES
================================================================================

Travelers implemented a new roof evaluation program in January 2025 called "RoofVerify DFW."
All new business submissions in Dallas, Tarrant, Collin, and Denton counties now trigger automatic
aerial inspection within 72 hours of bind. If roof condition is unsatisfactory, Travelers issues
a 30-day corrective notice, not immediate cancellation (more grace than Safeco).

Roof age rules (as of Q1 2026):
- 0-8 years: Full RCV, no photos needed, fast-track underwriting
- 9-16 years: RCV on Standard/Premier; require photos or RoofVerify report if home value $400K+
- 17-22 years: ACV only. Policy must include written acknowledgment from insured. Travelers issues annual rate adjustment on these policies.
- 23+ years: Ineligible for new business. Exception: metal standing-seam up to 35 years - call underwriting directly.
- Class 4 impact-resistant shingles (any age): Qualifies for 8% discount AND may push the age threshold 3 years forward (e.g., 19-year Class 4 roof treated as 16-year standard)
- Tile roofs (clay/concrete): Travelers writes these up to 25 years with inspection; common in North Dallas/Frisco new construction

Hail deductible options (unique to Travelers DFW):
- Standard: 1% of Coverage A
- Buy-down option: For an additional $180-$340/yr, reduce hail deductible from 1% to $2,500 flat (very popular with clients who have $500K+ homes)
- Enhanced: 2% deductible in exchange for 7% premium reduction (offer to budget-conscious buyers in Zone C/D)

WHAT TO AVOID - TRAVELERS
================================================================================

Do NOT submit to Travelers if any of the following apply:
- Roof age 23+ years (any standard shingle; call for metal exception)
- Three or more paid claims in past 5 years
- Any single water damage claim exceeding $50,000 in past 3 years (Travelers DFW has flagged water claims as a priority risk)
- Dog breed restrictions: Pit bull, Rottweiler, Akita, Wolf hybrid, Presa Canario (same core list as Safeco; Travelers also excludes Cane Corso and Dogo Argentino)
- Trampoline without full net enclosure AND signed exclusion (stricter than Safeco - Travelers requires BOTH)
- Vacant home (30+ days unoccupied): Requires Travelers Vacancy Permit endorsement; standard policy excludes vandalism/water if vacant
- Active mold claim or history of mold remediation in past 5 years: Underwriting will decline
- Knob-and-tube wiring: Hard decline
- Federal Pacific or Zinsco electrical panels: Decline (common in Dallas homes 1960-1985; ask about panel type)
- Active litigation involving the property
- Homes used for commercial cannabis cultivation or storage
- Polybutylene plumbing: Travelers will write with a plumbing exclusion endorsement (unlike Safeco which hard-declines); disclose to client

================================================================================
COMPETITIVE POSITIONING
================================================================================

Travelers wins against Safeco when:
- Home value $500K+ (Travelers' GRC terms and higher liability limits are better)
- Client needs umbrella continuity (Travelers umbrella up to $10M on one platform)
- Roof is 15-20 years old (Travelers' more generous age threshold)
- Inner Dallas older homes needing Ordinance/Law 100% (Travelers' 100% O&L vs Safeco's 50% on Premier)
- Client has one prior significant claim (Travelers underwriting is more flexible)
- Client has pool, guest house, or other amenities (Travelers liability packaging is stronger)
- Client cares about smart home water shutoff discount (Travelers' 7% is best in class)

Travelers loses to Safeco when:
- Client wants deepest home+auto bundle discount (Safeco/Liberty Mutual 18-22% vs Travelers 12-15%)
- Home is in north Collin County new construction (Safeco's base rates are lower in that zone)
- Client qualifies for Class 4 roof discount (Safeco's 12% beats Travelers' 8%)
- Speed matters - Safeco binds faster for clean risks (24 hrs vs Travelers 24-72 hrs)

Travelers wins against Progressive/Kemper when:
- Client wants carrier-direct claims service with local DFW adjusters (Travelers has a DFW claims office at 5080 Spectrum Dr, Addison)
- Premium home where A-rated carrier matters for lender requirements
- Jumbo mortgage lenders (over $1M) typically require AM Best A+ carrier - Travelers qualifies, Progressive may not satisfy some lenders

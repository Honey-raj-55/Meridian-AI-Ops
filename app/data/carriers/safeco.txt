================================================================================
COVERAGE TIERS
================================================================================

ESSENTIAL TIER
- Dwelling: Replacement Cost Value (RCV) with 20% extended replacement buffer
- Other structures: 10% of dwelling
- Personal property: ACV (actual cash value, NOT replacement cost)
- Loss of use: 20% of dwelling, up to 12 months
- Liability: $100,000
- Medical payments: $1,000 per person
- Deductible: 1% of dwelling value for named windstorm/hail; $1,000 all-other-peril
- No water backup included
- Roof: ACV schedule applies to roofs 11+ years old
- Best for: Price-sensitive buyers, rental/investment homes, clients who will not bundle

STANDARD TIER
- Dwelling: RCV with 25% extended replacement buffer
- Other structures: 10% of dwelling
- Personal property: RCV (replacement cost value)
- Loss of use: 30% of dwelling, up to 18 months
- Liability: $300,000
- Medical payments: $5,000 per person
- Deductible: 1% named windstorm/hail; $1,000 all-other-peril
- Water backup: $10,000 sublimit (included)
- Identity theft: $15,000 (included)
- Roof: RCV on roofs up to 15 years; ACV schedule kicks in at 16+ years
- Service line: NOT included, available as $6/mo add-on
- Best for: First-time homebuyers, median Dallas suburbs (Mesquite, Garland, Irving)

PREMIER TIER
- Dwelling: Guaranteed Replacement Cost (GRC) - full rebuild regardless of limit
- Other structures: 20% of dwelling
- Personal property: RCV, blanket $5,000 jewelry, $2,500 firearms, $2,500 silverware
- Loss of use: 50% of dwelling, 24 months
- Liability: $500,000 (umbrella-ready)
- Medical payments: $10,000 per person
- Deductible: 0.5% named windstorm/hail option available; $500 all-other-peril
- Water backup: $25,000
- Identity theft: $25,000
- Equipment breakdown: $50,000 (included)
- Ordinance/law: 50% of dwelling (CRITICAL for homes built pre-2005 in Dallas)
- Service line: Included up to $10,000
- Best for: Homes $400K+, custom builds, clients wanting one-stop premium coverage

TARGET CLIENT PROFILE - SAFECO SWEET SPOT
================================================================================

Ideal Safeco client at C1:
- Home value: $275,000 - $700,000
- Roof age: 0-12 years (metal or architectural shingle preferred)
- Credit score: 680+ (Safeco uses LexisNexis insurance score; below 640 expect surcharge or declination)
- Claims history: Zero claims in past 3 years; one paid claim in past 5 years acceptable if under $8,000
- Occupation: Professional, educator, first responder, military (all qualify for SafeConnect discount)
- Auto bundle: Safeco rates are 18-22% better when home+auto are bundled through Liberty Mutual/Safeco
- Construction: Frame, brick-veneer, or full brick; NO manufactured/modular homes, no homes on piers
- Distance to fire station: Must be within 5 road miles of responding fire station (most of DFW qualifies)

Dallas-specific notes:
- ZIP codes 75201-75215 (downtown core/East Dallas): higher theft score, expect 8-12% surcharge, redirect to Travelers
- North Dallas/Plano/Frisco/Allen: Safeco's best DFW territory, lowest base rates, cleanest loss ratio
- South Dallas, Oak Cliff, Pleasant Grove: Often hit FAIR plan thresholds; verify with underwriting first
- Flood zone AE or VE: Safeco writes dwelling but EXCLUDES flood; always pair with separate NFIP or Flood Simple policy

================================================================================
DALLAS HAIL AND ROOF-AGE UNDERWRITING NOTES
================================================================================

DFW is one of the highest hail-frequency markets in the country (avg 4-6 hail events per year exceeding 1" diameter).
Safeco has tightened roof guidelines significantly since the 2023 and 2024 storm seasons.

Roof age rules (as of Q1 2026):
- 0-10 years: Full RCV, no restriction. Quote freely.
- 11-15 years: RCV available on Standard/Premier only. Essential forces ACV. Require roof inspection report or photos at binding if Standard/Premier.
- 16-20 years: ACV only, regardless of tier. Mandatory roof condition photos submitted to underwriting before bind.
- 21+ years: Safeco will NOT write new business. Existing renewals get 90-day notice to replace. Do NOT quote these.
- TPO/flat roofs on residential: Ineligible regardless of age.
- Metal standing-seam roofs: Preferred. Safeco will write up to 30 years age with RCV on Standard/Premier.
- Class 4 impact-resistant shingles: Qualifies for Safeco hail-resistant discount (see discounts section). Verify with manufacturer cert.

Hail surcharge zones in DFW:
- Zone A (Lowest): Collin County (Plano, Allen, McKinney, Frisco) - base rate, no surcharge
- Zone B: Denton County, northern Dallas County - 4% surcharge
- Zone C: Southern Dallas County, Tarrant County, Ellis County - 9% surcharge
- Zone D (Highest): Hood, Johnson, Parker counties - 14% surcharge; refer to underwriting

WHAT TO AVOID - SAFECO
================================================================================

Do NOT submit to Safeco if any of the following apply:
- Roof age 21+ years (any material)
- More than 2 paid claims in past 5 years
- Any single claim exceeding $30,000 in past 5 years
- Dog breed exclusion list: Pit bull, Rottweiler, Doberman, Chow, Akita, Wolf hybrid (Safeco will flat-out decline or exclude liability)
- Trampoline on property without a net enclosure (Safeco requires signed trampoline exclusion or net cert)
- Swimming pool with no fence (must meet Dallas ordinance: 48" fence with self-latching gate)
- Short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO) activity - Safeco EXCLUDES business pursuits; use Travelers or add CBIZ endorsement
- Home-based business with customer foot traffic
- Homes with knob-and-tube wiring or fuse box (not circuit breaker) - automatic decline
- Homes with polybutylene plumbing (identifiable by gray plastic pipes, common in Dallas homes built 1978-1995)
- Unrepaired prior damage visible in photos (Safeco uses aerial imagery - EagleView or Verisk - on every new submission)

COMPETITIVE POSITIONING
================================================================================

Safeco wins against Travelers when:
- Client wants one carrier for home + auto (Safeco/Liberty Mutual bundle is tightest integration)
- Home is newer construction in north Dallas suburbs (Safeco's north DFW rates are 5-10% below Travelers)
- Client qualifies for Class 4 roof discount (Travelers discount is smaller at 8% vs Safeco's 12%)

Safeco loses to Travelers when:
- Home is high-value ($800K+) - Travelers has better GRC terms above $700K
- Client is in inner Dallas ZIP codes with elevated theft/liability scores
- Prior carrier non-renewal for claims (Travelers is more forgiving with one major claim)
- Client needs umbrella over $1M (direct Travelers umbrella is cleaner; Safeco requires separate Liberty Mutual umbrella policy)

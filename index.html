How It Works

From call to action in four steps.

Every inbound call runs through the same pipeline — analysis, contact resolution, integration events, and an audit trail.

01

Analyze the call

Gemini reads the transcript and returns structured JSON — client, urgency, needs, and flags.

02

Resolve the contact

The client is matched to the CRM contact record. Email is sourced from your data, never invented.

03

Queue integration events

HubSpot lead, advisor task, follow-up email, SMS, and MyUtilities handoff — all ready to send.

04

Log the audit trail

Every run is persisted to SQLite with a call ID, timestamp, and full JSON payload for compliance.
